PROJECT STATEMENT

BEYOND
FULLNESS.

Our project begins with a fundamental principle: 'The right to food is a human right.' Yet, as UNICEF Goodwill Ambassador George Perris reminds us, 'Malnutrition is not just lack of food. It is a lack of opportunity for life. Every child deserves to grow up healthy, strong and with dignity—and that is something we must all work together to achieve.'

Connecting these two truths reveals that the right to food cannot simply mean the right to an empty-calorie meal that temporarily fills a stomach—it must mean the right to adequate nutrition. Today, billions suffer from hidden hunger, feeling full while remaining deprived of the essential nutrients needed to learn, grow, and thrive.

Through our interactive website, we challenge the misconception that fullness equals nourishment and expose how poor nutrition silently robs individuals of their human potential. To turn this awareness into real impact, we invite visitors to challenge the myth that fullness equals nutrition, share the truth to defend food as a human right, and choose nutrient-dense food over empty calories.
